## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-trust, authoritative digital framework tailored for public governance, municipal administration, and smart city services. The visual expression is built around institutional clarity, functional legibility, and high-contrast usability for citizens across wide demographic and device spectrums.

### Personality & Values
- **Authoritative & Legitimate:** Projects the stability, dignity, and reliability expected of an official sovereign portal.
- **Accessible & Transparent:** Prioritizes WCAG AAA compliance, scannable hierarchies, and friction-free interaction models for civic utilities, citizen grievances, permits, and declarations.
- **Institutional Rigor:** Avoids decorative ornamentation, fleeting design trends, or excessive motion in favor of structural clarity, predictable navigational paths, and dependable informational density.

### Design Movement: Institutional Modernism
The style draws from contemporary civic systems and rigorous administrative design. It leverages crisp boundaries, deliberate typographic scale, quiet surface layering, and restrained semantic accents to maintain solemnity, efficiency, and unquestionable trust.

## Colors

The palette balances authoritative deep naval tones with sharp structural neutrals and unambiguous functional state indicators.

### Primary & Accent Roles
- **Primary Navy (`#0B2545`):** Used for primary actions, navigational headers, institutional banners, and dominant focal points. Communicates stability, authority, and official identity.
- **Secondary Royal Blue (`#1D4ED8`):** Serves as an interactive accent for hyperlinked text, active tab rules, focus rings, and high-priority directional indicators.
- **Tertiary Teal/Emerald (`#059669`):** Reserved for approved verification statuses, payment successes, valid service submissions, and certified credential indicators.

### Backgrounds & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#F8FAFC`):** The foundational neutral canvas for pages, offering a calm, glare-free working area that contrasts with pure white containers.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Applied to cards, modal panels, application forms, and data tables to preserve distinct functional zones.
- **Dividers & Structural Borders (`#E2E8F0`):** Defines rigid boundaries without adding visual clutter.

### Semantic Tones
- **Warning Amber (`#D97706`):** Highlights application renewal notices, pending documentary verifications, and non-blocking administrative warnings.
- **Critical Red (`#DC2626`):** Strictly restricted to error states, rejected submissions, urgent civic notices, and destructive confirmation steps.

### Typographic Contrast
- **Heading & Primary Body (`#0F172A`):** Deep slate/navy ensuring a contrast ratio exceeding 12:1 against light surfaces.
- **Secondary & Caption (`#475569`):** Neutral slate providing legible context for secondary metadata, helper text, and form requirements.

## Typography

The type system pairs **Public Sans** for display and headline structures with **Inter** for core reading surfaces and interactive form layouts.

### Structural Roles
- **Public Sans (Headings):** Designed for institutional digital interfaces, providing robust proportions, square curves, and unambiguous character boundaries that command respect and clarity in formal announcements and section breaks.
- **Inter (Body & Controls):** Delivers clean readability across dense tabular views, instructional copy, multi-stage application forms, and high-density citizen dashboards. Tall x-height and neutral geometry prevent visual fatigue during prolonged data entry.

### Editorial Hierarchy Rules
- Maintain fixed line-height proportions to prevent accidental overlaps in multilingual or translated regional contexts.
- Restrict uppercase transformations exclusively to micro-labels, status pills, and institutional codes (`label-sm`). Never apply uppercase styling to long civic headlines.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a structured, predictable 12-column fluid grid system designed for document-heavy pages, citizen service catalogs, and data-dense dashboards.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters, centered container capped at `1280px` max-width, with `2rem` outer margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, fluid width with `1.5rem` outer margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (`16px`) gutters, `1rem` outer canvas margins. Form controls and action buttons reflow into full-width structures.

### Structural Spacing Rules
- Element stacking strictly conforms to the `0.25rem` (4px) base scale.
- Compact groupings (input fields with accompanying helper text, card header badges) employ `space-xs` to `space-sm`.
- Distinct form fields within the same section use `space-md`. Major section demarcations within application records require `space-xl`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through structural borders, clear surface color contrast, and restrained elevation. Heavy shadows and decorative blurs are explicitly avoided to preserve institutional clarity.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Background tone (`#F8FAFC`), entirely flat.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Form Containers, Panels):** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a subtle 1px border (`#E2E8F0`). A light, crisp shadow provides soft separation: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdown Menus, Popovers, Action Menus):** Pure white background, `1px solid #CBD5E1`, with elevation: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs, Emergency Citizen Alerts):** Layered over a semi-transparent scrim (`rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.5)`), elevated by `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.

### Border Guidelines
Subtle, neutral borders anchor elements cleanly against canvas surfaces. Avoid colored glowing borders unless signaling a focused input or severe validation state.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, functional geometry (`roundedness: 1`), standardizing on subtle `4px` (0.25rem) corner radii for standard inputs and actionable components.

### Implementation Guidelines
- **Controls & Form Elements:** Buttons, text fields, checkboxes, and select pickers utilize `rounded` (0.25rem / 4px) to retain an authoritative, structured profile without severe hard edges.
- **Cards & Data Panels:** Containers, identity panels, and data tables apply `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px) with `1px` borders to soften large information layouts.
- **Badges & Micro-Pills:** Status badges may adopt fully rounded pill forms only when demarcating quick-glance status tags (e.g., "Active", "Verified", "Under Review").

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#0B2545`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `4px`. On hover, background lightens to `#133863`. Active state shifts to `#081A31`. Focus state renders a sharp 2px offset ring in `#1D4ED8`.
- **Secondary:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`. On hover, background shifts to `#F1F5F9`.
- **Tertiary / Subdued:** Transparent background, text `#1D4ED8`, padding aligned with surrounding text, underline on hover.
- **Critical:** Background `#DC2626`, text `#FFFFFF`. Reserved strictly for destructive public service cancellations or irreversible record removals.

### Input Fields & Selects
- **Height & Spacing:** Standardized at `40px` height with `0.75rem` (12px) horizontal padding.
- **Borders & Fill:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, radius `4px`.
- **Focus:** Sharp `2px` ring in `#1D4ED8` with zero border collapse.
- **Labels:** Positioned consistently above the field in `Inter` 14px Semi-Bold (`#0F172A`), accompanied by red asterisks (`#DC2626`) for mandatory institutional fields.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Geometry:** Checkboxes use `4px` border radius; radio buttons are circular.
- **Sizing:** `18px × 18px` with `1.5px` border in `#94A3B8`.
- **Checked State:** Background `#0B2545` with crisp `#FFFFFF` check/dot mark. High visual contrast ensures unambiguous selection recognition.

### Status Chips & Badges
- **Success / Verified:** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, border `1px solid #A7F3D0`.
- **Warning / Pending:** Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, border `1px solid #FDE68A`.
- **Alert / Rejected:** Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, border `1px solid #FECACA`.
- **Informational / Neutral:** Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#334155`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`.
- **Typography:** `label-sm` (11px, 600 weight, uppercase tracking).

### Cards & Data Panels
- **Structure:** `#FFFFFF` fill, `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, `16px` or `24px` internal padding, `8px` corner radius.
- **Headers:** Optional structural card header divided by a light `1px solid #E2E8F0` baseline, featuring a `Public Sans` 18px bold title.

### Data Tables
- **Header:** `#F8FAFC` background with a lower border `1px solid #CBD5E1`. Text in `label-sm` (uppercase, slate `#475569`).
- **Rows:** Zebra striping is avoided in favor of clean `1px solid #F1F5F9` row dividers. Row hover transitions gently to `#F8FAFC`.
- **Cell Padding:** `12px` vertical, `16px` horizontal, supporting high scanning density for records, applications, and logs.
